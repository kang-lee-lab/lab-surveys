Welcome to the Average Stress Quotient (ASQ) Calculator.

The Average Stress Quotient (ASQ) is a synthesized value derived from 29 Heart Rate Variability indexes (see description for each index in the table below). Heart Rate Variability indexes are measures of variation in the time intervals between two successive heartbeats. These measures can be obtained through analysis of an electrocardiogram. Extensive research has found these measures to be indicative of a person’s stress level. However, research often use different indexes, making interpretation of Heart Rate Variability as a physiological indicator of stress difficult. Wu et al. (submitted) established a method for synthesizing multiple indexes to enable examination of six dimensions of Heart Rate Variability, called Stress Quotient1-6 (SQ1-6). As well, as a single value called Average Stress Quotient (ASQ) is derived to allow easy interpretation. Here, we provide the ASQ Calculator for you to try Wu et al. (submitted)’s method! Please input values of Heart Rate Variability indexes and we will generate your SQ1-6 and ASQ!
